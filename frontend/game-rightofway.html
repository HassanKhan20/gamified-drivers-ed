Right-of-Way Sequencer

Four cars at a 4-way stop. Click them in correct departure order. Rule: whoever stopped FIRST goes first. If two cars stopped at the same time, the one on the RIGHT goes first (the other yields to the right). 100 pts per round, -25 per misclick.

How to play (open me first)
  1. Each car shows its arrival time in seconds when it stopped (smaller = arrived earlier).
  2. Click the car that should go FIRST, then the next, etc.
  3. Tie-breaker: if two cars arrived at the same second, the car on the RIGHT goes first. This is "yield to the right" — TX Transportation Code §545.151.
  4. Right turns clear faster than straight or left turns when arrival times match.
