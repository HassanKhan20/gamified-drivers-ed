/ Principles

What we will and won't do.

01

One charge. Forever.

You pay $29–$129 depending on your course tier. That's it. No subscription. No "complimentary" roadside-assistance trial that auto-converts. No re-bill if you take longer than 30 days.

02

State minutes from real engagement.

Texas requires 32 instructional hours for the teen course. Other schools enforce that with per-slide timers (60–90 seconds locked) and call it compliance. We track active engagement: scenarios, decisions, mastery quizzes. Idle = paused. The total still hits 32 TDLR hours, you just don't sit watching a countdown.

03

Public anything that's auditable.

The full course tree, time-credit map, refund policy, regulator filings, and pass-rate (once we have one) live on the public site. If a regulator audits us, the same information they ask for is what students already see.

04

Bilingual is not a translation layer.

A Auto, NHSA, Aceable all sell "Spanish drivers ed" — usually a Google-translated text dump. Goodlane ships native voice instruction in Spanish, scripted by a licensed bilingual TX instructor, with cultural framing ("imagínate que estás en una glorieta en Houston"), not a dictionary swap.

05

Parents are copilots, not invigilators.

Most "parent dashboards" are a glorified gradebook. Goodlane gives parents a behind-the-wheel logbook, daily scenario prompts to coach during real drives, and a clean weekly progress digest. The product turns drives into teaching moments, not just minutes-completed audits.

06

If it can't pass TDLR, we don't ship it.

Every course goes through TDLR curriculum review and licensed-instructor co-authoring before public sale. We do not market a course as state-approved before it actually is — full stop.
