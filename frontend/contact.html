The refund policy. In writing.

  • 14-day full refund. Inside 14 days of purchase, request a refund by email — we issue 100% back to your original payment method, typically within 3 business days. We do not require an explanation.
  • No restocking fee. No "non-refundable processing charge." No partial refunds. The full amount comes back.
  • No clawback if you complete the course. If you finish the course inside the refund window and pass, the certificate is yours to keep — we still refund. We're betting that doesn't happen often, and even if it does, the brand is worth more than $59.
  • No surprise re-bills. One charge, ever. We do not bundle "free roadside assistance" trials that auto-renew at $5/mo. We do not enroll you in anything you didn't pick.
  • Chargeback? If your bank reverses a charge, we don't deactivate your course access while we sort it out. The student is who matters; the payment dispute can wait.
  • Past 14 days? Email us. If you have a real reason — duplicate purchase, wrong course, illness — we still refund.
