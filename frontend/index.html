FAQ

The questions we get every week.

Don't see your question? Email hello@goodlane.example.

Is this TDLR-approved?

The curriculum is built around the TDLR Program of Instruction guide and 16 TAC §84.501 minimums (32 hours, time floors, identity checks, mastery thresholds). Provider approval is in progress; certificates are issued only after that's final. You can take the course now; the certificate ships the day approval lands.

Why parent-taught?

Texas lets a parent or guardian be the instructor of record. We supply the curriculum, you supply the supervised driving hours. It's the cheapest legal path and the most flexible — no driving school appointments.

How long does it take?

32 hours of classroom content (TDLR minimum) plus 30 behind-the-wheel hours your parent supervises. Most teens finish the classroom side in 4–6 weeks of evenings. The behind-the-wheel takes longer because you can only drive so much in a day.

Is the simulator a real driving test?

No. The DPS road test happens at a Texas DPS office, in a real car. Our simulator builds the muscle memory: signal first, full stop at the bar, hold three seconds, no curb-hits, no speeding. By the time you're at DPS, the moves are second nature.

What if I don't finish in one sitting?

That's the whole point. You can stop and resume anywhere. The chapter timer pauses when you switch tabs. Your streak runs whether you do one chapter a day or five — but five is rare and we tell you to slow down.

Can my parents do this with me in Spanish?

Yes. Every chapter, every quiz, every drill is fully Spanish. Toggle EN↔ES anytime from the top right. Parent-supervisor materials are also bilingual.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't run ads. We don't track you across other sites. The only third party that sees your info is the payment processor at checkout. Read the privacy policy — it's short and in plain English.

How do I get a refund?

If you haven't started the course yet, full refund — email us. Once you've started, we follow the TDLR refund rules in 16 TAC §84.502.
