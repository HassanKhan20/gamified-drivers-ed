Welcome to your simulator.

This is a 3D driving practice ground. Pick a scenario in the top-right when you're ready — Free Drive to roam, or Checkpoint Run / Road Test for guided exercises.

1
Keyboard: W or ↑ = accelerate. S or ↓ = brake. A D or ← → = steer. Q = left signal. E = right signal.
⚑
You're being graded. Each scenario checks for things like a full 3-second stop at the line, signalling before turns or lane changes, staying under the speed-limit sign on the left, and not hitting anything. The end card shows what you missed.
2
Touch (mobile): tap and hold the THR bar (right side) to accelerate, the BR bar to brake. Drag a finger left/right anywhere on the screen to steer.
3
Follow the green flagpoles. The compass at the bottom of the screen always points at your next target. The coach card on the bottom-left tells you what to do next.
4
Camera: C toggles 1st/3rd person. R restarts the current scenario. Stuck? Hit EXIT top-left to go back.
